Refund Policy

A clear approach to refunds for digital marketing, creative, advertising, and development services.

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

1. General Approach

Refund eligibility depends on the service, project stage, work already completed, third-party costs, and the specific terms agreed for the project. We aim to handle refund requests fairly and based on the actual status of the work.

2. Services Generally Refundable Before Work Starts

If a service fee has been paid but the agreed work has not started, a refund may be considered after any clearly disclosed payment-processing or third-party costs are accounted for, subject to the agreed project terms.

3. Services Generally Non-Refundable After Work Starts

Once substantial work has started—such as campaign setup, creative production, strategy work, development, configuration, or other agreed deliverables—the related service fee is generally non-refundable because time and resources have already been committed. Specific project terms may provide a different arrangement.

4. Advertising Spend

Advertising spend paid to third-party platforms such as Meta, Instagram, Google, TikTok, or other advertising systems is separate from Digit Buzzer Agency service fees. Platform charges are not automatically refundable by us. Any platform-level refund, credit, or billing adjustment is governed by the platform's own policies and decisions.

5. Project Cancellation

If a project is cancelled after work begins, we may calculate any amount due based on completed work, committed resources, approved costs, and the agreed project terms. Unused funds, where applicable and genuinely refundable, will be reviewed as part of the request.

6. Advertising Performance

A refund is not guaranteed merely because a campaign produces fewer leads, sales, followers, revenue, or ROAS than expected. Advertising performance depends on many factors, including budget, audience, competition, creative quality, offer, platform algorithms, tracking, and market conditions.

7. Third-Party Purchases and Costs

Domain names, hosting, software subscriptions, paid media, stock assets, platform fees, payment-processing charges, and other third-party purchases may be subject to the relevant provider's own refund rules.

8. How to Request a Refund

Send a refund request through our official Contact Us page or WhatsApp at +92 348 6588922. Include your name, project/service, payment information available to you, reason for the request, and relevant project details.

9. Review and Decision

We will review the request against the service status, work completed, third-party costs, and any agreed project terms. If a refund is approved, the method and timing will be communicated with the decision.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be discussed directly with Digit Buzzer Agency through WhatsApp or the Contact Us page.
