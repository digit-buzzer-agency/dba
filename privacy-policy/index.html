Privacy Policy

How Digit Buzzer Agency handles information connected with this website and our contact channels.

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive information such as your name, business name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, service requirements, project details, and messages you choose to send.

2. Website Usage Information

This website is primarily a static information and portfolio website. We may receive basic technical information through hosting or infrastructure providers, such as request data, browser information, device information, and IP address, where those providers make it available for security and operation.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to inquiries, discuss services, prepare proposals, communicate about projects, provide requested services, maintain security, and improve our website or business processes.

4. Contact Form

The current website contact form prepares your inquiry and opens WhatsApp with the information you entered. It does not submit your form to a Digit Buzzer backend database. Once you continue the conversation through WhatsApp or another platform, that platform's own policies and practices apply.

5. Cookies

The website does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or behavioral profiling. Some third-party resources or hosting infrastructure may use their own technical mechanisms. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may link to or use third-party services and platforms, including WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Threads, Google Fonts, Unsplash-hosted media, and GitHub Pages hosting. We do not control the privacy practices of independent third parties. Review their respective policies before submitting information to them.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable practical steps to protect information we handle. However, no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain business communications and project information only for as long as reasonably necessary for communication, service delivery, records, or other legitimate business purposes. Retention may also depend on the platform through which you communicate with us.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold in our direct business records, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask us to stop using information for a particular communication purpose where practical. Any information held by a third-party platform is subject to that platform's own procedures.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services and website are intended for businesses and general audiences. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children through this website.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or information practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Digit Buzzer Agency through WhatsApp at +92 348 6588922 or use our Contact Us page.
